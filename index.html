Methodology & NASA Open Data Sources

1. What & Where is Changing? Evaluates multi-decadal time series from NASA GRACE/GRACE-FO (Terrestrial Water Storage), Terra/Aqua MODIS MOD13A2 (Vegetation NDVI), NASA AIRS (Surface Air Temperature), and NSIDC/NASA Sea Ice Index.

2. Why Do Opposing Trends Occur? A single planetary driver—such as Polar Amplification or El Niño-Southern Oscillation (ENSO)—triggers opposite local responses (e.g., Arctic sea ice collapse vs. Antarctic regional sea ice expansion, or Sahel regreening vs. Amazon drought browning).

3. Statistical Significance: Uses the non-parametric Mann-Kendall trend test ($p < 0.05$) and Theil-Sen median slope estimator to separate true directional climate signals from natural interannual noise.
